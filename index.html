        What is VORTEX//PLAY

        VORTEX//PLAY is a digital game storefront: weekly deals, a sales chart and fresh releases on one page. Every game has a page with a description, specs, screenshots and reviews, opened by clicking its cover. Purchased games go to your personal library, where you can launch them, see hours played, achievement progress and disk space. Every session is saved on the History page.
